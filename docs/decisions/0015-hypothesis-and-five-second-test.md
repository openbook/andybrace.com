# The page has to say what, who for and how to reach me within 30 seconds

**Decision.** The hypothesis is that a first-time visitor can say what I do, who it's for and how to get in touch within about 30 seconds. Before launch, 2 or 3 people do a 5-second test: they see the page for 5 seconds and then say what the person does and who for.

**Why.** That's the page's main job. If a visitor can't answer those questions, nothing else on the page matters.

**Riskiest assumptions.** StyleX and Astro work together (proved on 16 Oct). Hosting and DNS move cleanly. Anyone reads the page at all.
